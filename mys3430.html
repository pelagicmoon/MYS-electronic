志太の浦の　朝漕ぐ船は　寄し無しに　漕ぐらめかもよ　何しこざるらめ

Inlet of Shida: / morning rowboats. / Without nearing land, it seems, / they row on indeed. / Why won't you come ashore?

** The last phrase, 何しこざるらめ, has also been read in certain sources as よしこざるらめ. This would change "Why won't you come ashore?" to something like "It seems that after all, you won't come ashore"... a question that interacts with the distance to a resignation that maintains the distance.
